# Gayzette design direction

Approved preference, November 1, 2026: the TV Watchlist is Patrick's favorite design in the paper. Treat it as the primary visual reference for future Gayzette work.

Preserve the warm-paper background, expressive serif masthead, fine rules, generous spacing, vermilion metadata, burgundy split cover and theatrical still-life artwork. Extend this visual family to new beats; do not replace it with generic cards, dashboard panels or a different masthead.

Navigation order: **TV Watchlist**, **Podcast Watchlist**. Keep the two beats adjacent on every page. The Podcast Watchlist's microphone illustration is a companion to the TV still life, generated with Runway from the same art direction.

Editorial behavior: original reporting with visible sources, clear release dates, an honest distinction between a guest's claims and independent evidence, and useful context beyond episode summaries. Never expose private listening notes or personal reflections.

Public page: `/podcasts.html`. TV: `/tv/`.
