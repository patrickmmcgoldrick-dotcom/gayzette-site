Aldabra's giant tortoises number nearly 183,000, the highest count recorded

From fewer than 1,000 in the 1800s to a stable population, though older surveys likely missed many animals.

Aldabra is a remote coral atoll, a ring of coral islands around a lagoon, in the Seychelles, and it is home to giant tortoises. In the 19th century their number fell to fewer than 1,000. In 1981 the atoll became a strict reserve.

A 15-year-old built a QR emergency-info tool after a friend fainted

Aanya Mansani of Texas made Emergen after a friend fainted at school and no one knew her medical details. Scan a QR code and it shows allergies, medications, conditions and trusted contacts, with "no app download or login" needed.
