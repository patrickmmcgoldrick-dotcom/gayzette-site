AI · Lead

A 501-billion-parameter coding model, free to download, with a permissive license

Reflection's Beam is its first open-weight model, and you can try it today through Ollama or OpenRouter.

Reflection, an AI lab, published the weights of a coding model called Beam on Hugging Face on October 16, under the Apache 2.0 license, which lets you use, change and sell things built on it.

Read the original at Reflection: Beam is available →

AI

Meta releases Muse Spark Open, an 816B model with 1M-token memory

Meta released Muse Spark Open, an open-weight model with 816 billion parameters in total and 42 billion active at a time. It handles images natively and has a 1-million-token context window, which is how much text it can hold in mind at once.

Read the original at Meta Research: Muse Spark Open →

AI

NVIDIA's Nemotron 3.5: a 120B vision model and a 24GB one

NVIDIA released two models on October 15. Nemotron 3.5 Super VL is an open 120-billion-parameter model that reads images as well as text; it is on Hugging Face, OpenRouter, ModelScope and as an NVIDIA NIM package.

Read the original at NVIDIA Blog: Nemotron 3.5 Super VL →

AI

xAI's Grok Voice 3.0 costs $0.08 a minute

xAI released Grok Voice 3.0, a speech-to-speech model: audio goes in, audio comes out, with no separate text step in between. It sits in xAI's Voice Agent API, and the grok-voice-latest alias switched over automatically.

Read the original at xAI: Grok Voice 3.0 →

AI

Claude now works inside Google Docs, Sheets and Slides

Until now, using Claude on a document meant a round trip: copy the text out, paste it into a chat, copy the answer back. Anthropic has moved Claude into the document.
